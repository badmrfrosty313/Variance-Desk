# Financial methodology

VarianceDesk stores monthly budget and actual amounts by department and account. Revenue and expense records remain separate so that operating income is always revenue less expenses.

For revenue, favorable variance equals actual minus budget. For expenses, favorable variance equals budget minus actual. A positive favorable variance therefore has the same management meaning in both cases. The percentage uses favorable variance divided by the absolute budget. It is blank when budget is zero.

Forecasts begin with the final historical actual month. Each scenario compounds monthly revenue and expense assumptions for twelve months. Optimistic and pessimistic adjustments alter revenue growth and apply a smaller, opposite effect to expense growth. This is a transparent scenario calculation, not a statistical prediction.

The demonstration data is fictional, deterministic, and safe to reset. Its management commentary names material numerical contributors only; it does not infer explanations from accounting data.
